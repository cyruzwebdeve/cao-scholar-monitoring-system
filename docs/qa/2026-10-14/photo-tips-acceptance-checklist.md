# Photo tips acceptance checklist — 2026-10-14

Use test documents and an authorized Admin account. Basic checks provide advice, not proof of readability or approval.

1. Open Scholar → individual drawer → Capture initial requirements. Capture a page with **Take photo**, **Open live camera**, or **Choose file** (JPG/PNG). Confirm **Tips for this photo** appears under the selected preview.
2. Capture a dim page. Expect advice to improve lighting when the sample is sufficiently dark. Capture a very small image (for example, 320×480); expect **Small image** and advice to move closer without cutting off edges.
3. Capture a well-lit, sharp page. Inspect the smallest text yourself. Depending on its layout, the card may report no obvious warnings or offer additional detail/lighting advice; neither result verifies the document.
4. Retake or remove a page. Prior tips must disappear. Switch pages/requirements and apply a crop or rotation; tips must describe the newly selected/processed preview.
5. Pair a phone using **Use phone camera** and sign in with the same Admin account. Capture a page on the phone; check the tip card before **Upload**. Advice must not disable Upload. After transfer, check the corresponding laptop preview and tips, then end the phone connection before final confirmation.
6. Confirm existing page ordering, PDF conversion and **Confirm, upload & approve initial set** still work after reviewing every document. A tip must never automatically approve a file.
7. On a narrow phone screen, check that the card fits, text wraps, and retake/Upload controls remain usable. PDF-only selections should not show a photo-quality claim.
